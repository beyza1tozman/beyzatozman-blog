I haven't read a lot of sci-fi books. I seem to read them very slowly and not finish them. Even though this one started as really fun and riveting, after nearly reaching the halfway point, I stopped for no reason. But when I finally picked it up again nearly two months later, I finished it in just 2-3 days.

The book starts with Ryland Grace waking up on a spaceship with no memory of how he got there or what his mission is. As he slowly remembers, we learn he's on a desperate mission to save Earth.

I enjoyed the second half much more. A lot of unexpected and surprisingly emotional moments happened, along with even more science. As I was reaching the end, loving it, I was scared I wouldn't like the ending, but it didn't disappoint and became one of my favorite endings to a book.
