It was an interesting read. I decided to read this after seeing a youtuber (Ruby Granger) talk about it in her 'My Favorite 25 Books of 2025' video, and I got intrigued. I also had never heard of this writer before. I liked his writing but the book overall was not something I usually read. I do like creepy, unsettling stories sometimes, but this was more on the poetic and abstract side, and I couldn't make sense of some of the stories, even though that's probably not what was aimed for in his book. I really enjoyed some of the stories and my top 3 stories are:

1. The Unstrung Harp
2. The Doubtful Guest
3. The Remembered Visit
