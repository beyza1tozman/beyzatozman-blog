It's the first book I have read by R.F. Kuang. I had been wanting to read one of her books for a long time, and when I heard that my friend was reading Yellowface, I got curious and decided to start with this one.

I really loved the plot of the book, and the first 100 pages went by very fast. It was interesting and thrilling, and not the kind of book I was expecting. But I didn't enjoy the second half as much, as the events started to feel repetitive and boring, and I think the ending was not the best way to close the story.

I finished some of the chapters by listening to the audiobook version, and I thought the narrator did a great job. Her voice is perfect for June, and she conveys her inner turmoil very well.
**[Audiobook link](https://open.spotify.com/show/1GtmeaFAf0AEQecONsLhYW?si=ec9e3f2240944779)**

If there is a second book, I would probably read it. I'd also like to read more from her in the future, as I liked her writing style.
