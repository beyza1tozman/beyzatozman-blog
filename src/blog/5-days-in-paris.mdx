## Intro

This was a very unexpected trip.

One summer day in 2024, while we were talking about places we wanted to travel to in the future with my friend, Paris came up. As we started talking about the details of this yet non-existent trip, like where to go, how it would go, and when it might possibly happen, we thought, why not in the near future? It occurred to us that if we talked with our parents and planned properly, we might actually go this year. Since she had gone on a Europe trip not a long while ago, I was more hopeful about my parents giving permission to go with her.
And they did! And I was to go on my first abroad trip without my family.

Then the preparations began. First off was buying plane tickets and booking accommodation. We thought of going in spring for a few days, and since it was still months away, we managed to get both the tickets and the reservation at good prices. We also had plenty of time to plan our days, researching places, making museum reservations, and buying tickets for Paris Disneyland.

The waiting process was both enjoyable and exciting. At the same time, I was a little anxious about any possible mishap, since I had missed a flight before. I just wanted the time of the flight to come as soon as possible, get on the plane, and arrive safely.

A few weeks before the trip, I experienced something interesting. A fun encounter. I was looking at novels in my university library, and since I like old hardcover books, I picked up a blue, old hardcover book with interest. Its title was 'Five days in Paris'. And since I was about to stay five days in Paris myself, I was delighted by the coincidence and borrowed the book.

## Day 1

Paris welcomed us with gloomy weather. We were very excited when we landed and wanted to get to our hotel as soon as possible, and then do the things we had planned for day 1, which were to see the Arc de Triomphe and Eiffel Tower.

We had a hard time understanding which transportation we needed to use to get to our hotel and what transportation card we needed to get. No one was speaking English at the airport, and a few times we went back and forth between the places they directed us to, wandering around the airport. The communication issues went on throughout the trip and were our biggest problem, but once we got our metro cards, we managed to get around on our own, with Google Translate helping when needed. 
After finally getting a weekly metro pass, we headed to our hotel. It was a little far from the city center, but it was a fair trade-off for the price. We put our stuff down, rested a bit, then went out again to officially start our first day.

Arc de Triomphe was our first stop. When we got there, it was raining a little.

<Image src={arcImage} alt="Arc de Triomphe"/>

Then we walked to the Eiffel Tower. Seeing it in person was beautiful. It was much taller than I expected. I had often heard people say they weren't too impressed by it, saying that it's just a tall metal tower, but I thought it was very nice.

As we walked near the tower, the rain got heavier and we got cold and wet.
We searched for a cafe or a restaurant where we could both escape the rain and eat something. We entered the first restaurant near us and shared an onion soup. This was at the top of the list of French food I wanted to try. Although I don't like onion that much, I liked this soup; it was very comforting on a rainy Paris day.

<Image src={onionImage} alt="French onion soup"/>

And even though we had more plans for that day, we decided to keep day 1 short and return to our hotel.

While walking to the metro, we noticed that many cafes were already closed, and learned that cafes in Paris close quite early. We saw one that wasn't closed yet and bought a few desserts to try in our hotel room before sleeping.

We didn't like them much, but still, they were a sweet ending to our first day in Paris.

## Day 2

This was the day we were going to Disneyland, and because Disneyland Paris is almost outside of Paris, we woke up early to be there by opening time.

After just one day of travel, I was already really tired. My feet were hurting badly, and it was totally on me. The shoes I wore all day yesterday were ones I hadn't worn for more than 2 hours outside before, and they turned out to be uncomfortable for long days. Luckily, I had packed another pair: long boots that were comfortable enough and suitable for the rainy weather. I was also a little sick, and my voice was kind of hoarse. It stayed that way for the following week, making it hard for me to talk.

We arrived at Disneyland on time, even a bit early, and started waiting in a long line. When we were finally let inside, we strolled around the cute Disney-themed boutiques a bit, then tried to decide on our first ride. We hadn't paid for the fast-pass option to bypass the lines, so we had to wait at least 20 minutes for all of them. But since we stayed there all day, we managed to both walk around and experience 5 rides.

At the end of the day, there was a big event. First, a drone show that lasted quite a while, and when it ended, I thought that was it for the day. But then an even longer fireworks show started. The whole show was a choreography featuring characters from different Disney movies. My friend and I even got a little emotional during the Tangled part since it is one of our favorite Disney movies.

While we were watching the fireworks, a kind French girl tapped me on the shoulder and showed me her phone, on which she had typed something in French and was showing me the translation: "Your bag is open. Keep it in front of you". I had already heard this advice many times before and was trying to be careful, I thanked her and closed my purse.

It was a lively day, and I was happy to cross something off my bucket list.

## Day 3

This day was for museums and parks.

We started the day at the Place de la Concorde. We saw the Fontaine des Mers and then walked to the famous park right next to it, Jardin des Tuileries. It was lovely. I learned that the Palace of Tuileries was burned in 1871. Only the garden remains, and it is free to enter all year.

Then we went to Musée de l'Orangerie. It was the first of the three museums we had booked months before for this day. I loved seeing all the artworks, especially Monet's, since he is one of my favorite painters. The museum also had a shop inside that sold souvenirs related to the art in the museum, and we got a few things.

<Image src={orangerieImage} alt="Musée de l'Orangerie"/>

After seeing Musée de l'Orangerie, we went to the Musée du Louvre. We spent nearly 3 hours in the Louvre and I became very tired. And I think you need a whole day to be able to see all of the Louvre properly. It is vast, and you have to stop in every corner to admire all the pieces. We also had one more museum we reserved for that day at 4, so we went out.

Final museum of the day was Dior museum. It was not within walking distance, and we had to take the metro. I became a little stressed about not arriving on time and not being let inside. So we rushed and arrived around 4:15. We waited in line for a short while, then thankfully entered without any problem. There were many stunning designs and dresses, and in one room, they were projecting different visuals onto the walls, which was really beautiful.

<Image src={diorImage} alt="Dior Museum"/>

After that, we were very hungry, so we went to a cafe nearby that we had saved. I had chicken with potatoes, and it was very good.

Later, we went back to the Louvre to take pictures from the outside. We took photos just before they closed the area.

<Image src={louvreImage} alt="Louvre"/>

We bought macarons from Ladurée and started walking to Notre-Dame Cathedral. The way there was a scenic 20-minute walk alongside the Seine River. When we arrived, there were people making music, and we sat in the area and ate our macarons. With the sun just set, the deep blue sky and Notre-Dame in front of us created a very beautiful scene. And the macarons were the best I have ever had.

<Image src={notredameImage} alt="notredame"/>

By this time, I was exhausted and wanted to go back to our hotel, but we also wanted to see the Eiffel Tower sparkling in the evening. We heard that it happens for 5 minutes on the hour from dusk until midnight. We asked a few people there to confirm, then decided to go there, hoping we would see it at 9 pm.

The Eiffel was a few stops away. And when we arrived, there was still some time left until 9 pm. There were many people selling tiny Eiffel Tower ornaments and keychains. We walked around and tried to find a nice spot that wasn't too crowded.

The sparkling Eiffel was mesmerizing and a great way to end the day.

<Image src={eiffelImage} alt="eiffel tower"/>

## Day 4

Today was going to be our kind of solo travel day. We had talked the day before and decided that on our final full day in Paris, after visiting the Palace of Versailles together, we would spend the rest of the day on our own. She wanted to go to more than one museum, and I wanted to have a more relaxed and spontaneous day.

We went to Versailles Palace. Its luxury and grandeur were striking. It also had a garden, but entering it required an extra ticket, so I decided not to enter and to move on with the other things I had planned for the day. And so my solo travel began.

I wanted to go to Musée d'Orsay, then to the Luxembourg Garden, and then decide what to do next.

I had a great time at Musée d'Orsay.

<Image src={orsayImage} alt="musee d'orsay"/>

Then I went to Luxembourg Garden. And I had a nicer experience there than at Tuileries garden because I got to sit and relax more on its benches and take it all in. While I was there, looking at my phone for what I might do next, I saw a recommendation for Shakespeare and Company bookshop. I remembered hearing about it before and decided it would be really nice to visit. It was within walking distance as well, so I could take my time walking there and explore along the way.

And I realized that I really like traveling this way: without following plans too strictly, just walking along the streets and checking out what intrigues you. I experienced 2 lovely things, experiences I wouldn't have had if I had just followed my plans.

First, I saw another really cute bookshop and strolled inside. They were selling Studio Ghibli merch as well. As a Ghibli lover who doesn't encounter this often in my home country, I was glad to come across these and bought a pair of Kiki's Delivery Service chopsticks.

Then I walked through a festive street with many vendors selling sweets. I bought a Belgian waffle, which I really wanted to try, and sat in a nice little park nearby to eat it.

After about 10 more minutes of walking, I reached Shakespeare and Company. There was a small line, and it had started to drizzle. The shop was very small, with a cozy atmosphere, and I loved it. I bought a small children's book as a souvenir, which they stamped.

<Image src={shakespeareImage} alt="Shakespeare and Company bookshop" />

I thought of going to Angelina as well after the bookshop to try their famous hot chocolate. But since I took a little too much time in the bookshop I had to rush to get there, scared that they might close. I arrived just in time. There was a small line, and after I started to wait with them, someone working inside came out and said that we were the last ones they would accept for the day and that we couldn't order any more food. I was fine with it since the thing I wanted to try was their hot chocolate.

<Image src={angelinaImage} alt="angelina cafe"/>

When I finally entered, I was seated at a lovely table. The interior was really charming. After they served my hot chocolate, the waiter also brought me a piece of chocolate. I liked the hot chocolate, but even with the whipped cream it felt too sweet to me. I think it would have been a lot better with something else to balance the sweetness.

I left at the time they closed the cafe, and it was raining harder than before. I tried to walk to the metro, then when I found an open cafe, I decided to wait there for a bit until the rain eased up and decide what to do next.

I had 2 options: either I go back to the hotel and call it a day since it was already late, past 8 pm, or I go on a Seine River boat cruise.
I was feeling a little dizzy, so I was highly considering just going to the hotel, but after resting there for a bit and talking about the different tour boat options with the owner (which was the longest conversation I had with someone there in English), and as my dizziness had faded, I decided to go for the boat ride.

I went and bought tickets for what I think was the last ride of the day, at 9:30 pm. While I was waiting by the boat for the ride to start, under the Eiffel Tower, the tower started to sparkle. Just like yesterday, at 9 pm. And because I wasn't waiting for it, I got really surprised. It was still slightly raining, and seeing it sparkle in the rain felt beautiful in a different kind of way. And I was glad to see it one more time. Then the boat ride started, and it was very pleasant. They played many French songs, and it felt like the perfect way to end the trip.

While I was walking to the bus stop to go back to our hotel at around 11 pm, I saw that my friend was waiting there as well. After spending the day by ourselves, meeting at the end like that was very surprising.

## Day 5

The day of our return.

We woke up early, finished packing our things, and checked out of our hotel.

We arrived at the airport early and waited for a few hours. The flight went very smoothly. I was in the window seat this time, which was nice. I spent the time looking through the photos I had taken, listening to music, and sleeping.

<Image src={planeImage} alt="plane"/>

We arrived in Istanbul, then went to the train station. Here we were going to part ways with my friend. She was going to Konya and I to Ankara. We said our goodbyes and I got on my train and travelled for like 6 hours. I tried to sleep which was much needed since I was very tired. But I was also scared of oversleeping and missing my stop, so I couldnt sleep well.

Eventually, I arrived, and my family was there to meet me.

These were the longest and one of the most memorable 5 days I have ever had, and I am very happy that I got to experience it.
